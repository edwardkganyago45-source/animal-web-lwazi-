The Majestic Lion

The lion is often called the "king of the jungle" and is one of the most iconic animals in the world. They live in prides and are known for their strength, courage, and powerful roar.

Lions are social cats, spending much of their time resting together and hunting in groups. They play an important role in maintaining balance in their ecosystems.

Interesting Facts

  • Habitat: Grasslands and savannas of Africa.
